# Australia Through Birdwatchers' Eyes

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious Malaysian readers with no assumed knowledge of Australian birds, geography or seasons. Also reviewed on a small laptop by FIT3179 markers.

## Product Purpose

A single scrolling, evidence-led visual field journal about where citizen scientists record Australian birds, how the recorded mix changes through the year, and what the observations can and cannot tell us. More than ten charts, at least eight effective advanced idioms, and three distinct map idioms using Vega or Vega-Lite. GitHub publishing is the final stage.

## Brand Personality

Observant, tactile, surprising. The user chose a modern field journal with paper surfaces, bird illustrations and specimen-style notes. They want an exceptional visual result, supported by real data rather than complexity for its own sake.

## Anti-references

The selected direction is a guided field journal rather than the alternative dark observatory or premium nature editorial. The user selected focused interactions with the main findings visible while scrolling, rather than an exploration-heavy dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Source and validate data before selecting charts or writing conclusions.
- Every visual earns its place by answering a different question.
- Explain Australian geography and seasons for Malaysian beginners.
- Let readers see the story immediately; use controls for useful detail.
- Keep observations, population estimates, and conservation listings conceptually separate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation defaults: readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, reduced-motion support, labels in addition to colour, text summaries and downloadable tables. No horizontal page scrolling on small laptops. Responsive figures and a printable view.

## Confirmed scope

The user has not submitted a sketch and says their lecturer permits drawing it after the website. The user asked for both a complete 2024 seasonal baseline and a newer 2025–2026 view. The latter must visibly distinguish data coverage and ingestion delays.
